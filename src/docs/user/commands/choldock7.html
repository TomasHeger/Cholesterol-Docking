Command: choldock7

Automated rigid-body docking of cholesterol (PDB CCD component CLR) into multiple cryo-EM density sites.

Usage

choldock7 map-spec proteinModels protein-spec [options]

For parent models with child surfaces/pseudobonds, exact-parent selectors such as #!1 and #!2 are recommended.

choldock7 #!1 proteinModels #!2 detectionLevel 0.141 supportLevel 0.05 out C:/work/cholesterol_v7

Algorithm

V7 detects high-contour rod-like candidate density, estimates the membrane from a protein-subtracted and smoothed experimental map, rigid-body docks CLR, tests both head-tail orientations, performs weak-site rescue, and jointly optimizes neighboring rods to avoid a tilted ligand stealing a neighboring site.

Confidence

HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW are evidence tiers, not occupancy probabilities. Confidence uses density support, local mask occupancy, OH orientation, membrane association, neighboring-density overlap, rescue status, and ambiguity of the joint neighbor assignment.

Diagnostics

site_diagnostics.csv records generated/refined pose counts and rejection counts for protein clash, low support, outside-mask placement, inward OH, neighbor intrusion, and joint-optimization exclusion. The best rejected pose is summarized to help diagnose visually convincing unfilled sites.

Main options

  • detectionLevel absolute high-confidence map contour (default 0.141)
  • supportLevel lower map level used to support the full CLR (default 0.05)
  • orientations random PCA-guided starting poses per site (default 1500)
  • maxsites maximum candidate rods (default 60)
  • proteinMapResolution calculated protein map resolution for membrane subtraction (default 6 A)
  • blobSmoothing residual-map Gaussian smoothing sigma (default 3 A)
  • blobThresholdFraction positive residual threshold as fraction of maximum (default 0.10)
  • proteinMaskRadius remove residual density near protein for membrane segmentation only (default 2.5 A)
